01 / INTRODUCTION

Sylvia Odan, bringing alternative R&B and experimental music to the ears of the 21st century. A new sound that you've heard through your headphones. But now you get to experience it in person. Come live in EON's Universe.

02 / THE UNIVERSE

EON's Universe is not just a few songs recorded by a beautiful voice, but a series of creative ideas that go into creating an atmosphere of beautiful things. With the alternative style bringing in things that are out of the norm, to the coldness of the color palette. This universe is much more than you would ever imagine.

03 / A NOTE FROM SYLVIA
"When creating my EP, I knew that there was more than one way to tell a story bigger than just recording a song. I wanted to show the process of what was going through my mind. After that I wanted to immerse myself in a world, not only for me but my beautiful supporters. A world free of judgment, of hate, of evilness. Just a world to simply exist and love one another. So I started this EP, met some wonderful people who helped create this visual, and together we created EON's Universe."

— Sylvia
